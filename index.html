02 — Who & how

Who we are

A family, registered in two countries.

Members come from abuse, abandonment, neglect, or the loss of parents: toddlers to young adults, widows, and grandparents, including children who are deaf, nonverbal, disabled, or HIV positive. All raised to become Ugandan leaders.
